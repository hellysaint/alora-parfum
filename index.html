The Box

Every detail,
considered.

01

Magnetic-close, cotton-paper box

A rigid two-piece box wrapped in brushed cotton paper — blush on the inside, soft taupe out.

02

Hand-tied jute twine

Tied by hand in small batches, no two knots identical — a quiet signature of the atelier.

03

Foil-stamped ALORA seal

Our arch mark, pressed — not printed — into the lid.

Fitted felt cradles each 3ml vial, and doubles as a travel tray after your first order.
